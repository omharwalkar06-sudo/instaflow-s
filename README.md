# InstaFlow

**One upload. Thousands of accounts. Zero manual work.**

InstaFlow is a B2B SaaS platform that helps Indian brands and agencies scale their Instagram presence. Upload one reel — InstaFlow handles the rest.

---

## The Problem

Indian brands pay agencies ₹1,50,000–3,50,000/month to manually manage Instagram growth across multiple accounts. It's slow, expensive, and doesn't scale.

---

## The Solution

InstaFlow distributes content across large Instagram account networks instantly. Each account gets its own unique version of the content — different video, different caption, different identity.

---

## How It Works

**Step 1 — Upload**
Brand uploads one reel to the InstaFlow dashboard.

**Step 2 — Process**
InstaFlow creates a unique version of the reel for each account using AI and video processing. Every copy looks original.

**Step 3 — Distribute**
InstaFlow posts to all accounts simultaneously with unique AI-generated captions in Hinglish, English, or Hindi.

**Step 4 — Track**
Real-time analytics show views, likes, and comments across all accounts from one dashboard.

---

## What Makes It Different

- **Every reel is unique** — each account gets a different version. No duplicate detection.
- **AI captions** — Google Gemini generates natural Hinglish captions per account
- **Real Indian identities** — each account has its own Indian IP, Indian device, Indian behavior pattern
- **Human-like activity** — accounts browse, like, and follow daily to stay active and trusted
- **Real-time dashboard** — see every post, every metric, every account in one place
